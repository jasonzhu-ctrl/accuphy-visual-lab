## 0. The answer in one page

1. **Demand is not the bottleneck; the last step is.** In 17 months the store had 183k
   sessions, 5.8k add-to-carts and 5.0k checkouts, but only 452 completed. Canadians finish
   19 % of checkouts, Americans 4.8 %. In the US store **PingLive is not offered at all** (only
   the Mini is published for the US market), import duties are added at the last step, and
   orders ship from Canada. **Fixing US availability and checkout is the biggest, fastest
   lever** (for the Mini alone: ≈ 150 more US orders over the same period at a 12 % completion
   rate; ≈ 300 at Canada's rate).
2. **Accuphy is, in practice, a Canadian winter business.** 80 % of revenue is Canadian;
   November–February brought 43 % of all orders; Manitoba buys at 3.5× Ontario's rate per
   person. **The 2026 ice season is the year's make-or-break window, and it starts now.**
3. **Change the headline.** Garmin's LiveScope 2 (July 2026) has no black box either, so "no
   black box" is no longer ours. What no major brand offers is **"no display to buy: your phone
   or tablet is the screen"**. That makes PingLive a complete system for CAD 1,749 when
   LiveScope 2 costs CAD 2,799.95 *before* its display.
4. **Win the doubters on the phone, not the sonar.** Owners love the picture and the two-wire
   install. Non-owners doubt the tablet in the cold, battery drain, the app, and believe myths
   ("needs cell service", "it's Bluetooth"). Some first hear "from China". Every one of these has
   a factual answer that is not on the first screen today.
5. **Proof is thin.** No star ratings on the store, almost no app-store ratings, few English
   reviews. Social traffic converts at a fifth of search. An unknown brand at CAD 887–1,749
   needs reviews, unedited footage, a clear warranty and a face.
6. **Owned channels are idle.** The abandoned-cart email alone returned CAD 44.6 per recipient,
   but the list is ~1.7k people and no campaign has gone out since 1 May 2026.
7. **The Mini is the growth product and the weak spot.** It now outsells PingLive (Q3 2026:
   41 vs 20 orders) but is returned 2.6× as often (12.4 % of units). Expectation-setting (what a
   39° beam shows) is a sales lever, and it is exactly what the Accuphy Lab visualises.
8. **Europe is searching and cannot buy here** (≈ 25k sessions, almost no orders) while dealers
   now exist in Germany, France, Poland, the Baltics, Hungary, Finland and Ukraine.

**Recommended order:** fix the leaks (September–October) → win the ice season (October–February)
→ build proof and an owned audience all year → grow channels. Details in §6; each item is an
experiment in `experiments.md`.

## 3. Why they buy, and what stops them

**Jobs:** see fish and how they react, live; get LiveScope-level vision without a new display
or wiring; one portable system for boat, kayak, dock and ice; keep up with fishing partners;
enjoy it ("fun watching fish come in").

**Objections, ranked** (details and answers in `voice-of-customer.md` §3):

1. Whole-system cost vs LiveScope (they forget the display; we should not).
2. Phone or tablet in the cold; battery drain.
3. Unknown / Chinese brand; app privacy.
4. Distrust of phone apps.
5. Myths: needs cell service; uses Bluetooth.
6. Learning curve.
7. Ethics and tournament rules (strong in bass and musky circles).
8. Image quality of the Mini's forward view.
9. Mounting.

Accuphy's own retailer FAQ answers exactly the practical ones (pole mounts, TV mirroring, several
devices, Wi-Fi range, cell service and latency), and the two blog posts that earn traffic are the
setup guide and the Android compatibility list. The questions are known; the answers are buried.

## 4. Competitors and our position

Full table in `competitors.md`. The position that matters:

- **Every incumbent needs its own brand display.** LiveScope 2 (US$1,999.99 / CAD 2,799.95),
  MEGA Live 2 (US$1,599.99 / CAD 2,239.99; with an XPLORE 9 ≈ US$2,800), ActiveTarget 2
  (US$1,399.99 / ≈ CAD 2,380 + display). No major brand uses a phone or tablet as the screen.
- **The entry anchor is Lowrance Eagle Eye 9** (≈ US$915–999 with a 9" screen, but one narrow
  beam, usable to ~50–60 ft).
- **PingLive (US$1,262, 135°, three views, 240 ft) sits alone in the gap** between Eagle Eye and
  the ~US$2,800 systems. **PingLive Mini (US$699) sits below Eagle Eye.**
- **Canadian prices for incumbents run 1.40–1.70× US list**, so the gap is widest at home.
- **Ice bundles** from rivals cost US$2,750–3,300 complete (Humminbird, Garmin) or US$1,549
  without a screen (Lowrance).
- **Unknown:** low-cost Chinese live sonar (next round).

**Positioning proposal:**
> **PingLive — live sonar on the phone or tablet you already own.**
> A complete system for less than most brands charge for the transducer alone. One system for
> the boat, the kayak, the dock and the ice.

Proof points, in this order: real unedited screen footage → owner stories by region →
whole-system price table → 2-year warranty, support from Ontario, free app with no subscription,
firmware updates → the myths answered (own Wi-Fi, no cell service, not Bluetooth, up to three
devices, mirror to a TV) → who Accuphy is.

**Say:** "live sonar", "your phone or tablet is the screen", "no display to buy", "watch them
come in", "see how they react", "keep your graph, add live", "two wires". Name LiveScope honestly
when comparing. **Don't say:** "cheat", "edge", "sharpshoot muskies", "widest" (LiveScope 2 and
ActiveTarget 2 XL cover more), or lead with "no black box" (no longer unique).

**Origin:** tell it plainly before others frame it: founded 2020, engineering in Beijing and
Hangzhou, North American home, support and warranty in Oakville, Ontario (the About page already
says most of this). Don't claim "Made in Canada".

## 5. Where the sales leak (diagnosis)

| Stage | Canada | US | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sessions → add to cart | 3.1 % | **3.6 %** | Americans want it at least as much |
| Checkout → completed | **19.0 %** | **4.8 %** | The US loses buyers at the final step |
| What the US store offers | PingLive, Mini, 0° mount | **Mini only** (PingLive and the mount are not published for the US market) | The flagship, 74 % of store revenue, cannot be bought online by US visitors (38 % of sessions) |
| US market setting | — | USD price, **duties added at checkout**, taxes added, ships from Canada | Likely cause of the drop: a surprise at the last step |

Whether keeping PingLive out of the US is deliberate (distribution territory, legal review,
supply: 9 units in stock) is Jason's knowledge, not visible in the data. If it is deliberate,
the US web plan is Mini-only and the landed-cost fix applies to the Mini; US PingLive demand then
needs a US dealer or distributor.

Other leaks:

- **Proof:** no store reviews; app ratings near zero; social and YouTube visitors don't buy
  (0.06 % / 0 %). They need more proof before they are ready.
- **Messages:** the product page leads with "built-in processor / no black box / 50 % fewer
  wires": the one claim LiveScope 2 has now matched. The phone-as-screen advantage and the myth
  answers are below the fold or in the FAQ.
- **Facts:** public specs disagree (range 240 vs 300 ft; Mini weight and thickness; voltage;
  Wi-Fi range). The US store showed a French title in search results; "Ping" collides with a
  crypto token, PING golf and Blue Robotics in search.
- **Europe:** ≈ 25k sessions with no path to a local dealer.
- **After purchase:** Mini returns at 12.4 %; no review request; no referral ask.
- **Measurement:** 51 % "direct", 6 tagged paid sessions in 17 months: ads cannot be judged.

## 6. The plan

Each line maps to `experiments.md`; all are proposals awaiting Jason's go.

### 6.1 Fix the leaks (now – mid October)

1. **US availability and checkout (E1).** First Jason's call: should US shoppers be able to
   buy PingLive (today only the Mini is offered)? Then: place a test checkout for the Mini to a
   US address and write down what is added. If duties or brokerage appear, switch the US market
   to duties-included pricing (or ship US orders from US stock) and say "no surprise fees" on the
   product page and in the cart. Target: US checkout completion ≥ 12 %.
2. **First screen of both product pages.** Lead with "your phone or tablet is the screen: no
   display to buy"; add one myth line ("its own Wi-Fi: no cell service, no internet, not
   Bluetooth; up to three devices"); show warranty, Ontario support and delivery time.
3. **One official spec sheet** used by the store, FAQ, manuals and every dealer.
4. **Search hygiene:** fix the /en-us page title and duplicate pages; always write
   "Accuphy PingLive Live Sonar", never "Ping" alone.
5. **Tracking (E9):** UTM tags on every link we control, Meta pixel + Conversions API, and one
   post-purchase question: "How did you first hear about PingLive?"
6. **Pay over time (E2)** on both markets.

### 6.2 Win the ice season (October – February) (E5)

- **Ice kits:** Mini Ice Kit and PingLive Ice Kit (0° mount, pole, a battery that meets the
  11–18 V / 6 A rule, case), priced as a complete answer against US$2,750–3,300 rival bundles.
- **A cold-weather guide** with measured battery drain and tested temperatures, the shack setup,
  TV mirroring in the wheelhouse. It answers objection 2 head-on.
- **Where:** MB, SK, NW Ontario, QC, MN, ND, WI, MI (the data says so).
- **When:** early access for the email list in October, Black Friday week (27 Nov – 30 Nov
  2026), holiday gifting to mid-December, first-ice owner stories in January.
- **What to show:** unedited screen recordings from real ice; Lake Winnipeg and Lake of the
  Woods proof; the Mini's flasher view.

### 6.3 Build proof (all year) (E3, E11)

- Ask **all 434 past customers** for a review with a photo or screen recording before the
  season; show stars on product pages; in-app rating prompt after a good session.
- Unedited side-by-side footage against LiveScope at real depths (the test skeptics ask for).
- A "who we are" page: founders' story, teams, where support and warranty happen.
- Mini returns: tag the reason on every return for 30 days, fix the top two gaps on the page and
  in the welcome email (for example, what a 39° beam covers compared with 135°).

### 6.4 Grow the owned audience (E4, E6, E7)

- Checkout-abandon email v2 (three emails, objection-led).
- One useful email a month; browse abandonment; win-back; owner referral.
- **The Accuphy Lab as the top of the funnel** (§7): sign-up → five-part education series →
  offer.

### 6.5 Channels (E8, E12, E13, E14)

- **Dealers first, because they carry ~94 % of units.** Give them what makes a stranger trust a
  new brand: the unedited footage and review quotes from §6.3, the one spec sheet (E16), the
  retailer FAQ videos, kits (E17), a shared promotion calendar with consistent prices, and the
  Accuphy Lab as an in-store and on-site demo (Round 0 §7.8). Fast warranty handling matters
  more to loyalty than the faults themselves (Round 0 §4).
- **Europe:** send visitors to their local dealer.
- **Ice-hut pilot:** a PingLive Mini in rented huts on a few big ice lakes, with a QR offer.
- **Creators with codes:** ten mid-size walleye / ice / kayak creators in MB, SK, ON, QC, MN, WI
  (the Feb 2025 shortlist had six, two tied to Garmin).
- **Dealer demo days** in Manitoba, Saskatchewan and Ontario during the ice season; one promotion
  calendar shared with dealers so the store and dealers don't undercut each other.
- **No US dealer was found.** A US partner in the ice belt would also solve the duties problem.

### 6.6 Product and assortment

- Kits by use: Ice, Kayak, Boat.
- **PingBeam** (side / down scan, €389 in Germany) and **Router TWO** (two transducers on one
  phone) are sold in Europe but not in the North American store: an add-on for 434 owners.
- If "KingFisher 10" is a 10-inch screen, it answers the "phone screen in sun and cold" objection
  for boat anglers (to confirm with Jason).
- PingLive stock is 9 units going into the peak season.

## 7. How the Accuphy Lab fits

Round 0 §7 already proposed the Lab as "the friend's boat" (the see-it-working moment), the "aha"
as "live sonar on the phone or tablet you already own", an onboarding tool for reading the
picture, a dealer demo, and multi-device sharing; decision #53 added the Boat · Kayak · Ice
choice. This round's evidence agrees and sharpens three points:

1. **It demonstrates the differentiator, which is now "no display to buy".** Since LiveScope 2
   dropped the black box, the phone-as-screen is the one thing no rival can show. On phones the
   Lab plans to make the visitor's own phone the sonar screen (decision #47, principle 1): the
   page itself is the proof. No competitor has an interactive beam explorer.
2. **It sets expectations before purchase, not only after.** Showing what 39° (Mini) covers next
   to 135° (PingLive) at the buyer's own depth helps people pick the right model; the Mini's
   12.4 % return rate is the number to watch.
3. **It fills the email list** that the rest of the plan runs on (decision #45); the list is
   ~1.7k people today and idle since May.

On the open "aha" question (mobile plan, decision 3): Round 0 points to A, and this round's
competitor evidence agrees. For people who already know LiveScope, a second line works: "the
same live view, no display to buy". Jason decides.
