## 1. The short version

- **"No black box" no longer sets PingLive apart.** Garmin **LiveScope 2** (7 Jul 2026) dropped
  the box; Humminbird MEGA Live 2 and Lowrance Eagle Eye never had one.
- **"No display to buy" does.** Every incumbent needs **its own brand's display** (network port,
  firmware support). No major brand lets a phone or tablet be the screen.
- **The cheapest brand-name live sonar is Lowrance Eagle Eye 9** (≈ US$915–999 with its own 9"
  screen, but one narrow "keyhole" beam, usable to ~50–60 ft). It anchors what buyers think
  "entry live sonar" costs.
- **Between Eagle Eye (~US$1k) and mainstream systems (~US$2,800+) there is almost nothing.**
  PingLive (US$1,262, 135°, three views, 240 ft) sits in that gap; PingLive Mini (US$699) sits
  below Eagle Eye.
- **Canadian prices for incumbents are 1.40–1.70× their US list**, so the gap is widest in
  Canada, Accuphy's core market.
- Tournament trails now limit forward-facing sonar (MLF from 2026; B.A.S.S. reportedly 5 of 9
  events [U]). Pros are a weakening endorsement channel; recreational, ice, kayak and family
  anglers are the market.

## 5. How they market

- **Spec superlatives:** Garmin "clearest live sonar yet"; Lowrance "surface-to-surface 180°",
  "industry-first 360° Scout XL"; Humminbird palettes with fun names ("Citrus Shad", "PB&J").
- **Launches timed to events:** January (Miami show, AT2 XL), March (Bassmaster Classic, Garmin
  360°), July (LiveScope 2), **mid-September ice bundles** (Humminbird, 2025-09-16), December
  (MEGA Live 2 before the holidays).
- **Upgrade paths keep owners in the brand** (AT2 XL reuses the AT2 module; LS Plus transducers
  reuse GLS 10 boxes).
- **Education is left to retailers and press** (FishUSA comparisons, Carolina Sportsman
  compatibility charts, "which one should you buy" posts).
- **Promotions:** retailer gift cards (Scheels US$400 with a Garmin ice bundle), markdowns
  (MEGA Live 2 at 1,399.99; Eagle Eye at 915), dealer-exclusive ice bundles.
- **No interactive beam visualizer was found** (not searched for specifically). If none exists,
  the Accuphy Lab is a first in the category (as `docs/research.md` §7 also found in 2026-09).

## 6. Threats to watch

1. Incumbents getting simpler and cheaper (no box, LS Plus at 1,499.99, sales on MEGA Live 2,
   Eagle Eye at 915).
2. A wider-beam, low-cost Eagle Eye successor would hit Accuphy directly (speculative).
3. Tournament limits and an "FFS is unfair" story reaching regulators (Eagle Lake, Ontario:
   voluntary musky pledge).
4. Tariffs and currency on the US side (Garmin expected ~US$100M of 2025 tariff costs).
5. The premium bar rising (360°, auto-aim, stabilisation).
6. **Unknown low-cost Chinese phone-based FFS** [GAP].
7. A used-LiveScope wave as owners move to LiveScope 2 (Kijiji already lists LiveScope units).
8. Phone-as-display risks: cold, glare, battery, OS updates, Wi-Fi reliability.
