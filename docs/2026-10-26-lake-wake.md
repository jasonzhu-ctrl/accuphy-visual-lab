# Lake: the drag cue on iPhone, and the boat's wake (2026-10-26, decision #166)

Jason looked at the friends' test link on his own iPhone (GitHub Pages bd1e13e, built from Lab de00e3e) and said:
"船划过的地方的波纹看着还是非常奇怪。还有就是 drag the boat 那个标签啊，这个船四周这个标签。在船动过一次以后，它就不出现了。"
Lab HQ dispatched both points as LAB-iphone-fix.

## 1. The drag cue never came back on a touch screen

**Cause, found and reproduced:**
- React Three Fiber never passes a `pointercancel` on to the mesh. Its handler only clears hover state; see
  `handlePointer('onPointerCancel')` in `@react-three/fiber`.
- iOS cancels touches readily, for example when it briefly takes the gesture for itself.
- After a cancel, `dragging` stayed `true` forever. The boat counted as "still being dragged", so the reticle and
  the caption never returned.
- The #160 check missed this because it used a desktop browser at 375×812 with a mouse.

**Fix:**
- `PickPlane` (Scene.jsx) ends the drag on any `pointerup`, `pointercancel`, `touchend` or `touchcancel` on the
  window, and on blur or a hidden page.
- The ice page's `IcePick` (IceCamp.jsx) drops its aim ring on a cancel. A normal tap works as before.

**Checked:**
- Chromium with real touch input (CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, 390×844, mobile):
  - Before the fix, a touch ended by `touchcancel` left `dragging = true` and the cue stayed hidden for good.
  - After the fix, the cue is back 1.2 s after the boat stops, whether the touch ends normally or by cancel.
- WebKit, Safari's engine (390×844, mobile, touch):
  - A drag released normally brings the cue back.
  - A drag ended by `pointercancel` brings it back too.
  - Playwright cannot send a touch drag to WebKit, so this test used pointer events.
- Still owed: a check on a real iPhone.

## 2. The wake

**Before** (frames at 390×844, `docs/2026-10-26-lake-wake.jpg`, top row), what read as wrong:
- The arms were a ladder of evenly spaced, broad bars, about 1.5 m apart.
- A string of white beads ran along one arm. These were the foam "tops" on each crest.
- Transverse bars sat behind the stern.
- The arms stayed sharp for 2 s or more after the boat had stopped, so they looked painted on.
- The wake map stretched up to 80 m across in 256 texels. That is 0.3 m a texel, too coarse for fine crests, so they
  came out as smooth bars.

**What real small boats show** (`docs/2026-10-26-lake-wake-refs.jpg`, all from Wikimedia Commons):
- The arms are many fine crests that curve, like feathers. Each arm has a sharp outer edge, and the crests die away
  toward the middle.
- A calm, smoother lane runs down the middle.
- White water appears only in a short, ragged trail at the stern.
- No beads, no rungs.

**After** (middle row):
- **Slick:** a calm lane where the sea's ripples are damped by 75 %, a little brighter. It lasts about 4 s.
- **Arms:**
  - Thin, with a sharp outer edge on the 19.5° line, dying away inward.
  - Fine crests, about 0.9 m apart, whose angle turns from 35° at the edge to square toward the middle, so each
    crest curves.
  - About 10 m long, gone about 2 s after the boat stops.
- **Foam:** ragged white water only behind the stern, gone in about 1.5 s.
- **Speed:** every part scales with the speed that stretch of path was laid at, so a slow nudge leaves next to nothing.
- **Removed:** the transverse bars and the beads.
- **Wake map:** covers only the newest 26 m of path, at most 44 m across, at 384². That is at most 0.12 m a texel.

**Frame time (#50):** headless laptop GPU, vsync off, interleaved builds of HEAD and of this change, 2 runs each.

| | old, still | old, wake | new, still | new, wake |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phone 390×844 @3 | 2.45–2.56 ms | 2.50–2.51 ms | 2.24–2.33 ms | 2.39–2.69 ms |
| Desktop 1920×1200 | 5.16–5.51 ms | 5.25–5.29 ms | 5.07–5.13 ms | 5.28–5.42 ms |

There is no measurable change; the differences are within run-to-run noise.

**Checked:**
- Summer lake on phone and desktop: the cue comes back after a drag, and the wake fades.
- Ice page on phone and desktop: a tap still drills a new hole. The ice page has no wake.
- 0 console errors.

## Open question for Jason (not done; it changes the sonar)

The boat never turns. Its bow always points the same way, because the sonar beams, and the Forward view in
particular, are fixed to that direction. Drag the boat toward the camera or to the left, and it goes backwards or
sideways, with the wake coming out of the bow. In every real photo the wake comes from the stern. This is likely part
of "非常奇怪" too.

Turning the hull toward its travel direction would also turn the beams and the sonar picture with it. That is a
bigger change to the sonar engine.

**Options for Jason:**
- **(a)** Turn the boat and the beams together. This is the most real; it is bigger work, and the whole lake and the
  ice page need a QA pass.
- **(b)** Keep it as it is. The wake now looks right in any direction.

## 中文摘要（给 Jason）

- **拖船提示：** 找到原因了。iPhone 有时会“取消”一次触摸，我们用的 3D 库收到取消时不会告诉船，所以船以为自己一直被拖着，提示就再也不出来。现在不管手指是正常抬起还是被系统取消，提示都会在船停下约 1 秒后回来。在模拟触屏和 Safari 的引擎上都测过；还要你在真 iPhone 上看一眼。
- **船尾波纹：**
  - 以前：像梯子一样的一条条横杠，还有一串白珠子，停船后还挂很久。
  - 现在照真实小船的航拍改了：中间一条平静的水道，两边是细细的弯弧形波纹，船尾一小段白浪；停船约 2 秒就散。
  - 速度没受影响。
  - 对比图：`docs/2026-10-26-lake-wake.jpg`。
- **要你决定：** 船头永远朝一个方向，不会转。所以往镜头方向或往左拖时，船是倒着、横着走的，波纹从船头冒出来，这也是“奇怪”的一部分。要让船头跟着走的方向转，声纳的扫描方向也得跟着转，改动比较大。做不做由你定。
